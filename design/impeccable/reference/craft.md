# Craft (deprecated alias)

`craft` is a deprecated alias for an ordinary request to make new visual work. It adds no setup, no interview, no checkpoint, no tool, no quality behavior. You follow the normal routing in SKILL.md. You create a missing PRODUCT.md through [init.md](init.md). Then you follow [new-work.md](new-work.md) for visual authority, world and surface decisions, implementation, and finish.

Do not tell your user they need to invoke `craft`. Natural requests such as “build this feature,” “make a landing page,” or “redesign this screen” use the same flow. You hear the job in plain words. You do the same sound work.
